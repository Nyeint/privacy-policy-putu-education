Privacy Policy

PuTu – ပုတု (Android package: com.litobird.putu)
Effective date: 17 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 17 October 2026

PuTu ("we", "us", or "our") provides the PuTu mobile application (the "App"), an educational app that helps children learn the Myanmar and English alphabets, numbers, shapes, vocabulary, poems and stories through lessons, audio and games.

We care about the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why, and the choices you have.

In short: PuTu does not require an account and does not ask for a name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location. Learning progress and scores are saved only on your device. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may process limited device information as described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for or collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts or files from your device
  • Precise or approximate location (GPS)
  • Payment information

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. The only permission it uses is internet access.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

To make the App work smoothly, the following is saved locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Learning progress and game scores
  • App settings, such as language choice and whether onboarding has been shown
  • A temporary copy (cache) of lesson content, images and audio so lessons load faster

The App also checks your device's screen size to choose the right layout for phones or tablets. This check happens on your device and is not stored or sent to us. Text-to-speech features use your device's built-in speech engine.

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Information Processed When the App Loads Content

Lessons, images and audio are downloaded from our servers. Like any internet service, our servers and hosting providers automatically receive basic technical information needed to deliver content, such as your IP address, the type of request, and the date and time. We use this only to deliver content, keep the service secure, and fix problems. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with other information to build a profile of you.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, and information about ad interactions.

Because PuTu is designed for children, we configure ads to be treated as child-directed, which means ads should be non-personalised (not based on a user's interests or behaviour) and limited to content appropriate for children. We do not share any information with advertisers ourselves.

You can learn more about how Google uses information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

PuTu is made for young learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App does not include chat, user profiles, sign-up or any feature that allows children to share personal information publicly.

The App contains links (for example, to our Facebook page or to email us) in the Settings area. We recommend that a parent or guardian supervise children when using these links. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent any information. Information is processed by third parties only as described above:

  • Google AdMob – to show and measure ads
  • Our hosting and content delivery providers – to deliver lessons, images and audio

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Server logs kept by our hosting providers are retained only for a limited time for security and troubleshooting, then deleted. Because we do not create user accounts, there is no account to delete. If you have emailed us and would like that correspondence deleted, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Content is delivered over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We take reasonable measures to protect our systems, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Third-Party Links

The App may link to external services such as Facebook or your email app. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on our Facebook page.
